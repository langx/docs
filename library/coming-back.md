# 🔑 Coming Back From the Old App

The new LangX is a new app with a new sign-in. Your old password does not
carry over, but everything else does: your username, your profile, your
languages, your day streak, your token balance and your conversations with
anyone who has also come back.

## How to get in

Use the **same email address** you used before, and either of these:

- **Reset your password.** On the sign-in screen tap _Forgot password?_, enter
  your old email, and follow the link we send you. Choose a new password and
  sign in with it.
- **Continue with Google or Apple.** If that account uses the same email
  address, it signs you straight in.

Do **not** create a new account. Your old profile belongs to your old email
address; a fresh account under a different address would start from zero.

## What you will see

The first time you sign in, a _Welcome back_ screen lists what came back:
your @username, your streak, your converted token balance and a welcome-back
bonus, plus any conversations that were restored. If you had a large token
balance in the old app you may also find a lifetime plan waiting.

## Something missing?

- **No reset email?** Check spam, and make sure it is the address you used in
  the old app — a different address has no account behind it.
- **A form asked for your details?** Some old profiles were incomplete. The
  form is pre-filled with what we had; finish it and the rest is restored.
- **A conversation is gone?** Chat history only returns once **both** people
  are back. It appears on its own when the other person signs in.

Anything else, write to [hi@langx.io](mailto:hi@langx.io).
