# 🥇 Badges

A badge marks something you have already done. It is worked out from numbers
the app keeps anyway — your longest streak, how many corrections you have
written, how many tokens you have earned — so a badge can never disagree with
your profile, and it can never be taken away: every one of them counts
something that only goes up.

Badges **do not pay tokens and do not multiply them.** An earlier version of
LangX described badges with token multipliers; none of that carried over. How
tokens are earned is in [How Tokens Are Earned](../token/distibution.md).

## The ladders

| Badge         | Earned at                                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Streak        | A longest streak of 7, 30, 100, 180, 365, 730 and 1,095 days |
| Feed          | 1, 10, 100, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000 and 25,000 in the feed      |
| Messages      | 100, 1,000, 10,000 and 50,000 messages sent                  |
| Tokens earned | 10,000, 50,000 and 250,000 tokens earned                     |
| Member        | One, two and three years as a member                         |

- **Streak** badges read your **longest** streak, not your current one, so a
  broken streak does not cost you a badge you already had. The days are the
  same ones that pay a [streak bonus](day-streaks.md#milestones), so the badge
  screen and the token ledger always tell the same story.
- **Feed** badges count your posts and your corrections together: every post
  you have up, every correction you have written in a chat or on a post, and
  every recording you have left on a pronunciation request. It is the same
  number as the Feed tile on your profile. 5,000 of them, with a 365-day
  streak, is also what the Aurora frame asks for (see
  [Spending Tokens](../token/utility.md)). A deleted post leaves the count.
- **Tokens earned** counts what you have earned over all time, never your
  balance. Spending tokens on a frame cannot cost you a badge.
- **Member** badges are for staying rather than for effort — three years of
  turning up is reachable where three years without missing a day is not.

## Early Adopter

One badge nobody new can earn: **Early Adopter** is on every account that was
on LangX before the current app. It has no ladder and no progress bar — you
were there or you were not. If you had an account in the old app, see
[Coming Back From the Old App](coming-back.md).

## Where they show

Your badges are on your profile, with the next one on each ladder and how far
you are from it. You can share any badge you have earned as a card.

When you earn one, LangX tells you in an evening round-up rather than a buzz
each time — see [Notifications](notifications.md).
